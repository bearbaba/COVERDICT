SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT AN INSURANCE POLICY OR REAL COVERAGE.

Purpose: demonstrate whether an issuer-defined claim can be accepted by
independent consensus after checking transaction provenance and whether an
incident narrative matches predefined terms.

Covered: direct nonzero native ETH transfers by the claimant's own wallet into
the exact designated deposit address, during the predefined incident window,
only if a separate immutable issuer incident report explicitly confirms that
such transfers are covered by the simulated reimbursement program.

Excluded: transfers by other wallets, wrong destination, zero-value or failed
transactions, transfers outside the incident window, indirect transfers,
ERC20 transfers, alleged losses without on-chain confirmation, and all
real-world monetary claims or compensation obligations.

An Ethereum transaction can prove that ETH moved to a contract; it does NOT
prove that the sender lost funds due to a protocol exploit. Eligibility in
this example relates only to simulated demo-credit awards.
